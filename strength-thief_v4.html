Strength Thief

Every foe wears a number. It's a bluff. Their real power is hidden — you win only if your strength beats their real strength, and beating them steals half of it.

• Move with WASD / arrows. Touching a foe starts a fight.
• The number over a head is displayed strength — inflated by a hidden 0.5–1.0× multiplier.
• Win if your strength ≥ their real strength. Beat them → steal half. An exact tie still kills — but steals nothing.
• Foes hunt you and can ambush in packs. Every level resets you to 10 — no snowballing.
• From level 3, grab timed power-ups (5s each): ⚡ surge · 🛡 shield · ❄ freeze.
• Big foes are slow, lurking brutes; small ones are fast and eager — bait the giants once you're strong.
• Levels open with a 3–5s countdown + a brief grace beat. Clear a level → heal +1 life. 5 levels, 3 lives.
