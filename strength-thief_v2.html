Strength Thief

Every foe wears a number. It's a bluff. Their real power is hidden — you win a fight only if your strength beats their real strength, and beating them steals half of it.

• Move with WASD or arrows. Touching a foe starts a fight.
• The number over a head is displayed strength — inflated by a hidden 0.5–1.0× multiplier.
• Win only if your strength > their real strength. Win → steal half their real power.
• Foes hunt you. Weaker ones are lunch; stronger ones will corner and ambush you.
• Every level resets your strength to 10 — no more snowballing. Clear all foes to advance.
• 5 levels: foes get faster, hit harder, bluff less, and gang up more. 3 lives.
